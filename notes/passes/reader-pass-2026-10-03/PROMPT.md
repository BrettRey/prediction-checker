You are a reader, not a reviewer. Another model wrote this paper. Read-only; full report in your final message; state which model you are. Standard: truthful, fair, clear. Venue: Econ Journal Watch (readers: economists; not specialists in Canadian broadcasting).

Manuscript: `notes/passes/reader-pass-2026-10-03/main.txt` (text of the built PDF, commit f23c64e); LaTeX in `main.tex`, `sections/*.tex` if you need to check a float or reference.

Read the paper ONCE, in order, from the title to the end of the appendix tables, as a reader meeting it for the first time. Do not skip ahead. Do not fix as you go; take notes per section and keep going. The paper was restructured today; defects of this kind are created by revision, so look for them especially.

For each section (and subsection), note:
1. What does the reader know entering it, and what does it assume they know? Flag every term, symbol, quantity, acronym or reference used before it is introduced (e.g., "the band", "the designated error scale", "the partial recalculation", "k^u", "BYOP", "CPE", "the survey path").
2. What does it introduce, and is it used again? Flag notation or terms defined and never reused.
3. What does it promise, and where is that delivered? Flag promises (forward references, "below", "section X") not kept or kept somewhere else.
4. Does it have a shape, or is it a list?
Also, for every sentence that opens a paragraph or follows a heading: does it make the reader resolve something ("the second", "this", "the comparison", "these", "that gap")? If yes, quote it and say what it should name.
Also flag: transitions that assume a paragraph that is no longer there; an argument that arrives before its motivation; a claim stated in one place and contradicted or qualified differently in another; the same point made more than twice.

Report: per-section notes (brief), then a consolidated defect list ranked by how much each costs a first-time reader, each with location (section and quoted phrase), the defect, and a proposed fix. When you find one instance of a pattern, list every instance in the paper.
